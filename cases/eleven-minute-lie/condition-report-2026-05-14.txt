CONDITION REPORT — "The Blue Hour", Elin Varga, 1911, oil on canvas, 64 × 86 cm
Accession: 1987.112
Examined: 2026-05-14 by R. Okafor, collection conservator
Photographs: IMG-0514-01 to IMG-0514-09 (restoration archive). Overall view: IMG-0514-07.

Purpose: Pre-transfer documentation ahead of surface cleaning at an external studio.

Support and ground: Stable. Minor tension undulation at lower right corner.
Paint layer: Generally sound. Fine age craquelure throughout, typical for the period.
Varnish: Moderately discoloured. One distinctive varnish fracture in the upper-left quadrant of the sky: a single fine diagonal line, roughly 4 cm, running down and to the right from about one third of the way in from the left edge, with two short side branches. Stable; no lifting.

Recommendation: Surface clean and varnish reduction only. Do NOT consolidate or retouch the upper-left varnish fracture. It is a stable, well-documented feature and serves as the primary visual identification reference for this work (see IMG-0514-07).

Transfer: Scheduled 2026-06-02 to Lindqvist Conservation Studio under crate CR-04.
