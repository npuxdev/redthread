WITNESS STATEMENT — Tomas Reyes, security officer (badge G-44)
Taken by: police liaison, 2026-11-06 22:20

I came on at seven. My second round of the evening started from the control room a little after nine. I went straight to Gallery 2 and badged in at the main entrance, about 21:09.

When I came in, display case D-3 was already empty. The case door was closed and the glass was not broken. Nobody was in the room. I walked up to the case to make sure, then radioed the control room that The Blue Hour was not in the case. While I was on the radio, the gallery lights went out. The cameras stayed on; I could see the red lights on them.

I did not see anyone in the corridors on my way. I did not go into Corridor B; I don't have a key for the Gallery 2 service door. Officer Nakamura came to Gallery 2 later, after the lights had gone.

I don't know when the painting was taken. It was in the case on my first round, before eight.
