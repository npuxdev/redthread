INCIDENT REPORT — Loss of "The Blue Hour" (E. Varga, 1911), Gallery 2
Submitted by: Dr. Miriam Achterberg, Director
Reported by telephone to police: 2026-11-06 21:18. Written report: 2026-11-06 22:40.

At 21:12 the Gallery 2 lighting failed. I believe the theft was carried out under cover of that outage. Camera 04 shows display case D-3 empty at 21:16, after the lights failed and after Officer Reyes entered Gallery 2 at 21:09. As far as the records show, he was the only person in the room during that period. I have asked that he be suspended pending the investigation.

I was in my office in the administrative wing from about 20:15 until Officer Reyes's radio call reached me, and did not leave the administrative wing during that time. Earlier, at about 20:30, I walked past Gallery 2 and the painting was in place.

The Blue Hour is the most valuable work in the collection. It returned from conservation at the Lindqvist studio in July in excellent condition and was the centrepiece of the October reopening. The loss is catastrophic for the museum, and I have notified our insurer.
