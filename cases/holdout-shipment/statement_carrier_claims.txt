Shipment TA-SH-558214 / customer ref HX-26-0310
Author: Martin Lechner, Claims Manager          Date: 2026-03-13

TransAlp rejects liability for the shortage reported by Novak Handels.

1. Collection. Our driver P. Novotny arrived at Halvard Leipzig at 14:57 and the
   trailer was loaded and sealed (TA-4471893) by 15:10. The pallets were already
   wrapped and waiting at the door when he backed on. He did not handle the
   freight; loading was done by Halvard staff.

2. Transit to Plzen. The driver confirms he drove directly to our Plzen hub and
   made no stops. Seal TA-4471893 was checked intact on arrival at the hub.

3. Hub. The freight was weighed on intake at 242.6 kg and 253.8 kg. Our hub
   raised a weight exception against the CMR figure and emailed the shipper the
   same evening; we received no reply. The pallets were inspected visually and
   were never opened at the hub. They were weighed again before loading
   (242.6 / 253.7 kg) and sealed with TA-5530817.

4. Delivery. The consignee's own dock weights (242.7 / 253.7 kg) agree with our
   hub weights within scale tolerance. Nothing was removed while we had the
   goods. The shortfall against the CMR already existed when we received them.

5. Seal number. Seals issued to HUB PLZ are from series TA-5530800 to
   TA-5530899. Our seal numbers contain digits only after the TA- prefix. A
   reading of "TA-5530B17" is a misreading of TA-5530817. The consignee
   confirms the seal body was intact.

6. Wrap. Our hub does not rewrap customer pallets and holds no record of doing
   so for this shipment.

We request that the consignee redirect its claim to the shipper.

M. Lechner
