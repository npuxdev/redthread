FAC-911 — SecureGate gateway clock drift
Opened 2026-10-18 16:20 PDT; checked 2026-10-19 08:10 PDT.
SecureGate node SGW-2 is 11 minutes 00 seconds FAST relative to NTP (tested at 08:10 PDT October 19). Offset appears stable over the overnight interval according to heartbeat checks at 23:00 and 06:00. No correction was applied during the incident window.
To derive wall time from SecureGate device time during the incident, subtract 11 minutes. No evidence that IdP, job scheduler or DLP clocks share this drift.
